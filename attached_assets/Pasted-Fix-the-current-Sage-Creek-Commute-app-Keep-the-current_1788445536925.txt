3. FIX ADMIN COUNTING
====================================

Audit the admin dashboard because some counts appear wrong.

IMPORTANT RULE:

ONE completed questionnaire submission = ONE rider or driver.

Do NOT count:
- schedule rows
- weekdays
- arrival times
- analytics events

as separate users.

Example:

One rider selected:
Monday 10:30 AM
Tuesday 10:00 AM

Correct:
Total responses: 1
Riders: 1

But schedule stats can correctly show:
Monday: 1 active rider commute
Tuesday: 1 active rider commute

Arrival distribution can correctly show:
10:30 AM: 1
10:00 AM: 1

The dashboard must clearly separate:

UNIQUE PEOPLE
from
COMMUTE-DAY DATA.

All person-level stats should use unique completed response IDs.

This includes:
- total responses
- driver count
- rider count
- definitely/probably count
- driver compensation distribution
- rider willingness-to-pay distribution
- transportation method
- commute duration
- reliability
- dealbreakers

For schedule/day/time charts, multiple entries from one person are allowed because they travel on multiple days.
