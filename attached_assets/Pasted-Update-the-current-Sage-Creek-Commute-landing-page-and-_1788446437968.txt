Update the current Sage Creek Commute landing page and onboarding flow.

Do NOT redesign the overall style. Keep the current clean minimal look.

Main goal:
Make it extremely obvious that users must choose ONE of these two paths before starting:

- “I drive to U of M”
- “I need rides”

====================================
1. REMOVE THE TOP “CHECK YOUR COMMUTE” BUTTON
====================================

Delete the current top navigation button:
“Check your commute”

Reason:
It currently defaults into the driver flow, which can create inaccurate/fake driver responses.

There should NEVER be a generic CTA that automatically assumes Driver or Rider.

Any generic CTA must first ask the user to choose their role.

====================================
2. MAKE ROLE CHOICE MORE OBVIOUS IN HERO
====================================

Keep the hero buttons:

“I drive to U of M”
“I need rides”

But make the section clearer.

Add a short label directly above the buttons:

“First, choose how you usually get to campus”

Then show both buttons with equal visual importance.

Do not make one look like the obvious default.

Both should be similarly sized and equally prominent.

Suggested structure:

First, choose how you usually get to campus

[ 🚗 I drive to U of M ]
[ 🎒 I need rides ]

Small helper text below:
“We’ll ask different questions based on your commute.”

====================================
3. NO DEFAULT ROLE
====================================

Remove any code/state where Driver is automatically selected by default.

Initial role state should be:
null / unselected

The questionnaire should NOT start until the user explicitly selects Driver or Rider.

If someone somehow reaches the questionnaire route without selecting a role:
show a clean role-selection screen first.

Example:

“How do you usually get to U of M?”

[ I drive ]
[ I need rides ]

Do not assume Driver.

====================================
4. OTHER CTA BUTTONS
====================================

Audit every button/link on the public website.

Any CTA such as:
- Start
- Check your commute
- Get started
- Continue
- See if you match

must NOT automatically enter the driver questionnaire.

If the role is not selected yet:
the CTA should scroll to or open the Driver/Rider choice.

If the role is already selected:
then it can continue into the correct questionnaire.

====================================
5. ADD ROLE CHOICE AGAIN LOWER ON PAGE
====================================

Near the bottom of the landing page, before the footer, add another simple CTA section:

Title:
“Want to see if your commute could line up?”

Subtitle:
“Choose the option that describes you.”

Buttons:

🚗 I drive to U of M
🎒 I need rides

Again, equal visual weight.

This helps users who scroll past the hero.

====================================
6. ROLE INDICATOR DURING QUESTIONNAIRE
====================================

Once someone chooses a role, keep a small visible indicator near the top of the questionnaire.

Examples:

“Driver · Sage Creek → U of M”

or

“Rider · Sage Creek → U of M”

Add a small:
“Change”

link/button beside it.

If clicked:
- return to role selection
- warn/reset role-specific answers if necessary

This prevents someone from accidentally completing the wrong form.
